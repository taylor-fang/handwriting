Paper Hand

Turn a photo of your handwriting into a font. Print a sheet, write each letter in its box, take a picture, and download a font you can use in Paper or anywhere else. Everything happens in this page: your photo never leaves your device.

1Print the template

One sheet with a box for every letter and number, plus a few optional punctuation marks. It fits on Letter and A4 paper. Any print size works, as long as the four black corner squares are on the page.

No printer? Draw the grid by hand

You can rule the same grid with a pencil and ruler. The sizes matter, because the page cuts the boxes by measurement:

Corner squaresFour filled-in black squares, 8 mm on each side. Their centres form a rectangle 176 mm wide and 240 mm tall.
Grid8 columns and 9 rows. Measured from the centre of the top-left square, the first column starts 8 mm to the right and the first row starts 12 mm down.
SpacingEach column is 20 mm wide and each row 25 mm tall. Each box is 18.5 mm wide and 20.5 mm tall, centred in its column, at the top of its row.
BaselineA light line 13.5 mm below the top of each box. Letters sit on it.
OrderLeft to right, top to bottom: a to z, then A to Z, then 0 to 9, then . , ! ? ' - : ; ( )

Keep your ruled lines light (a pencil is ideal) so only your pen strokes count as ink.

2Write, then take a photo

Write each character in its box with a dark pen. Let it sit on the baseline, the way you would write on lined paper. Small letters reach about the dotted line; g, j, p, q and y hang below.

  • A black felt tip or gel pen works best. Pencil is too faint.
  • Fill the box comfortably, but keep every stroke inside it.
  • Photograph from straight above, in even daylight, with no shadow from your phone or hand.
  • Get the whole sheet in, with all four black squares. A scan works too.

3Upload and line up the corners

Drop your photo here, or

Drag each circle onto the middle of a black corner square (arrow keys nudge a focused circle). The outlines show where every box will be cut; they should sit on the printed boxes.

Using it in Paper

Paper's side bar labels use a font called Paper Hand, stored in one file: app/assets/PaperHand.css. Replace that file with the PaperHand.css you download here (keep the name), then reload Paper. The labels will be in your handwriting. The CSS file always calls the font "Paper Hand" so Paper finds it; the name above goes inside the font files.

Elsewhere, use the .woff with your own @font-face rule, or install the .otf on your computer.

Paper Hand is open source under the MIT license. It was made for Paper, a calm place to write. Fonts are built with opentype.js.
